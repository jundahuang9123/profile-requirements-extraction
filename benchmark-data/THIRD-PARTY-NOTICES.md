# Source attribution and reuse

The application repository's licence does **not** override source-document licences.
Source titles, authors where identified, URLs, versions and copyright information are
recorded in each `provenance/manifest.json`; original copies retain their embedded notices.
No source owner endorses this benchmark or its curator interpretations.

- **EU legal texts:** European Union; [EUR-Lex legal notice](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/content/legal-notice/legal-notice.html).
  Original dated CELEX texts are retained, rather than current consolidations.
- **W3C recommendations:** World Wide Web Consortium; copyright and document-permission
  notices remain in the original HTML. See each document's licence link.
- **DCAT-AP baseline files:** European Union/SEMIC. Historical ISA Open Metadata Licence
  v1.1 notices remain in PDFs; the pinned SEMIC/DCAT-AP repository also supplies CC BY 4.0.
- **StatDCAT/GeoDCAT:** European Union/PwC EU Services; ISA Open Metadata Licence v1.1,
  [licence reference](https://joinup.ec.europa.eu/category/licence/isa-open-metadata-licence-v11).
- **Public INSPIRE guidance:** European Commission/JRC, Rights: Public; source
  acknowledgement retained. No ISO standard is included.
- **Turning FAIR into reality** and **Cost of not having FAIR research data**: European
  Union 2018, EC Expert Group and EC/PwC respectively; reuse with acknowledgement is
  expressly authorised on their rights pages. Third-party exceptions remain applicable.
- **SPRINT federation paper:** Alessio Carenini, Andrea Fiano, Mario Scrocca, Marco
  Comerio and Irene Celino, 2021; [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **Understanding Metadata:** Jenn Riley, NISO 2017, © NISO;
  [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). This copy is for
  noncommercial PhD/research use. Do not treat the whole corpus as commercially
  permissive or silently relicense this file.
- **HealthData@EU project reports:** report authors and EHDS2 Consortium Partners;
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) explicitly declared in the
  later official-project Zenodo deposits. Deposit metadata is retained as licence evidence.
  Some PDFs contain older “all rights reserved” front matter; that discrepancy is recorded
  rather than erased. Curated observations and requirement synopses are adaptations.
- **JRC Data Policy:** European Union 2015; reproduction authorised with source
  acknowledgement, as stated on page 2.
- **mobilityDCAT specification source:** NAPCORE;
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) in the pinned repository README.
- **EPOS:** original repository MIT licence retained as `epos/provenance/EPOS-LICENSE.txt`.

NAPCORE's original report, CMC, SPRINT D2.3, the JRC workshop paper, DataCite 3.1 PDF,
TransportDCAT report and other works whose original redistribution rights were not
established are represented by citations or curator-written factual synopses. Download
availability, EU funding and hosting by W3C/GitHub do not by themselves establish a
redistribution licence. Synopses are not verbatim source transcriptions and are marked as
such. References to ISO or publisher articles do not include their full copyrighted text.
