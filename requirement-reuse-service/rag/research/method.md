# Literature-search method

Search date: 2026-07-15

## Question

Which expert perspectives and knowledge sources are needed to elicit, merge,
and criticise requirements for a construction-domain, DCAT-compatible metadata
profile used in federated data spaces?

## Search strategy

The review combined targeted Web searches with backward chaining from primary
specifications. Queries covered DCAT/DCAT-AP profiles, construction
classification and BIM, IFC/IDS/bSDD, AAS/IDTA, geospatial metadata, data-space
catalogues and policies, FAIR/provenance/quality, dataset discovery, ontology
engineering, and requirements quality.

Priority order:

1. Normative specifications and official standards-maintainer documentation.
2. Official implementation guidance and reference architectures.
3. Peer-reviewed original research and systematic surveys.
4. Project documentation from Construct-X and related data-space initiatives.

Wikipedia, vendor marketing, unsourced blogs, and social-media posts were not
selected as corpus sources. Search results could identify a lead, but the
catalogue records point to the primary or official destination wherever one was
available.

## Selection rules

A source was included when it supports at least one operational decision:

- distinguish reuse from extension;
- assign metadata to the correct DCAT resource;
- identify a construction discovery distinction;
- understand a construction exchange or semantic-identification mechanism;
- model provenance, quality, access, policy, or governance without inventing
  legal conclusions;
- assess publisher feasibility or consumer discovery utility; or
- consolidate and criticise requirements traceably.

Current, stable releases were preferred. Superseded sources are retained only
when historically important or when no later source replaces their method.
ISO catalogue pages are included as authoritative summaries; the full standards
are not redistributed.

## Limits

This is a focused scoping review, not a systematic review claiming exhaustive
coverage. The source catalogue should be version-reviewed before a formal study
freeze, particularly for DCAT-AP, AAS, Dataspace Protocol, Gaia-X, and DSSC
documents that continue to evolve.
