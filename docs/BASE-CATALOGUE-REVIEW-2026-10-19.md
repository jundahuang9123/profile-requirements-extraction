## Main decisions

- Replace malformed labels such as “has part”, “is version of of”, “serves dataset”, “update/” and “was generated by” with readable statements.
- Distinguish metadata-record listing/change dates from the publication/change dates of the described dataset.
- Distinguish access through a page/service from direct download, access permission from licence/rights policy, and planned availability duration from present access status.
- Distinguish geographic/temporal coverage from spatial/temporal resolution. Retain meters where the source specifies them.
- Distinguish a catalog’s classification scheme from a dataset’s assigned categories, and metadata language from data language.
- Preserve DCAT version snapshots separately from the broader editions/adaptations relation. Sequence predecessor/first/last do not become version relations.
- Retain the release-specific dataset-only topic in DCAT-AP 1.1; later releases retain the more general described resource.
- In DCAT-AP 3, describe the series establishment date and series update cadence independently of its members.
- Preserve inherited class/property contexts as they exist in the registry. A phrase review cannot silently remove inherited entries or reinterpret their normative applicability.

## Use in future runs

New runs and derived trials created on this branch automatically freeze the reviewed catalogue for their selected base. Select the historically correct base version for reproduction, or the intended current version for new profile work. There is no extra catalogue-import step.

Existing runs keep their saved catalogue and hash. Do not overwrite their saved catalogue or resume base classification after deploying a different catalogue: the existing guard refuses that drift. Use a separate derived trial when the earlier stages can be reused, or a fresh run when the extraction contract changes. Running services were not reloaded by this review.
